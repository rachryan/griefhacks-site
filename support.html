If you need urgent help

In Canada: if you are thinking about suicide or worried about someone else, call or text 9-8-8. Support is available 24 hours a day. Text 9-8-8 or read what to expect.

If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical help in Canada, call 9-1-1. Outside Canada, use the emergency or crisis service for your location.

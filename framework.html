The Grief Orientation Framework

A guide for finding your footing in a life that has changed.

Grief does not return life to what it was before. What can change is how you live inside it.

This guide brings together my perspective on orientation: choice, permanence, boundaries, and identity after loss. It reflects a personal approach, rather than a sequence everyone needs to follow.

If you are looking for a small, practical place to start today, the free support kit is here for you.

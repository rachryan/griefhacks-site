4 simple resets for hard mornings

By Momma R · 2 min read

Some mornings feel heavy before the day even begins. You wake up overwhelmed, tired, or carrying emotions you cannot explain. On those mornings, the goal is not to figure everything out. The goal is to steady yourself first.

1. Start with water

Wash your hands or face with warm water, or take a slow shower. Pay attention to the feeling of the water. Notice the temperature and the feeling of the water on your skin.

2. Ground your body

If comfortable, put your feet on the floor and notice the pressure beneath them. You can also notice the chair supporting you.

3. Slow your breathing

Take a slow breath in, then let your exhale be longer than your inhale. Repeat this a few times. Keep your breathing comfortable; skip this if it feels unpleasant.

4. Give yourself permission to go slow

You do not need to fix the heaviness right away. You do not need to have a productive morning. Tell yourself: “I can take this one step at a time.”

Remember this:

Hard mornings do not mean you are failing. You may be carrying a lot. Steadiness matters more than speed. A small action may make the next few minutes more manageable. It is also okay if it does not change how you feel.

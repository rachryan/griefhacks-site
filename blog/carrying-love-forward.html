Carrying love forward

By Momma R · 2 min read

Carrying love forward does not mean living in the past. It means letting the love you shared continue to shape the life you live now.

For me, love continues after a death, even as the way I carry it changes. It can show up in the way you speak, the choices you make, what you protect, and what you no longer accept. The people we love leave marks on us, and those marks continue long after they are gone.

Many people worry that keeping someone close means they are stuck in grief. But remembering someone and carrying them with you is not the same as being unable to move forward.

Sometimes love shows up in simple moments. In making a choice they would be proud of. In slowing down when life feels rushed. In choosing kindness, strength, or honesty because of what they taught you.

This is not living in yesterday. This is carrying their impact into today.

Continuing with life can include remembering them. You get to decide what that looks like for you.

You are not stuck because you remember. You are moving through life differently because you loved deeply, and that kind of love changes you forever.

A personal reflection. Your experience may be different.
